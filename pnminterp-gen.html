pnminterp-gen

NAME

pnminterp-gen - replaced by pamstretch-gen

DESCRIPTION

This program is part of Netpbm.

pnminterp-gen was replaced in Netpbm 9.21 (December 2001) by pamstretch-gen.

pamstretch-gen is backward compatible with pnminterp-gen, but also recognizes PAM input, including that with a transparency channel.
